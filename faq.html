Zero Online Advance Required! At SJT Tours & Travels, you do not need to enter credit cards or make online pre-payments. Simply submit your travel itinerary using our website form or WhatsApp us at +91 99009 75920.

Our operations team confirms your booking with driver and vehicle allotment details instantly. You only pay after your trip is safely completed directly to the chauffeur or through UPI/Bank transfer.

Our per-KM tariff includes the vehicle rental, fuel (diesel/petrol), and professional chauffeur service.

  • Driver Day Bata: Rs. 350 - Rs. 600 per calendar day (depending on vehicle size from Sedan to 50-Seater Coach).
  • Tolls & Parking: Payable at actual Fastag tolls and destination parking counters as incurred.
  • Interstate State Tax (if crossing state borders): Paid at actual RTO border checkposts (e.g., entering Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, or Goa).
  • No Hidden Costs: Clean transparent odometer readings from start hub to return.

As per standard South India commercial transport guidelines, outstation trips operate on a per-calendar-day minimum KM slab:

  • Sedans & SUVs (Etios, Ertiga, Innova Crysta): 250 KM per calendar day.
  • Tempo Travellers (12, 17, 20 Seater): 300 KM per calendar day.
  • Mini Buses (21, 25 Seater): 300 KM per calendar day.
  • Luxury 50-Seater Coaches: 350 KM per calendar day.

If your total trip distance exceeds the cumulative minimum allowance (e.g. 3 days × 300 KM = 900 KM), extra distance is billed purely at the agreed vehicle per-KM rate.
